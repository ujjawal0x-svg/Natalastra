# Focused reading experience

Chart reading now starts with a combined, concise reading and optional depth. Focus choices: self (Sun/Moon), connection (Venus/Mercury) and work/direction (Sun/Saturn). Sign-specific editorial lenses, selected major aspects and known-time houses are shown with their numerical basis. Context includes friends/family as well as partners, and study/care/projects as well as paid work. This is deterministic editorial content, not a live LLM, validated psychological profile or human-reviewed consultation.

Unknown-time combination excludes provisional Moon and all houses. Positions still use the existing noon reference; there is no claim that every other placement is unchanged throughout the birth day. No automatic birth-time rectification. Individual Moon inspection retains its provisional warning.

Interactive controls: focus switch, open individual placements, brief/deeper view, optional narration, explicit source explanation, optional reflection experiment and view-local fit feedback. Feedback resets when chart inputs/focus/system/language change and is not sent to the server or used to change the chart. Narration uses the existing free voice implementation. Paid dossier access is unchanged.

Individual placement inspector splits the existing prose into a concise lead/caution plus optional aspect/generational context. Full original body remains available for existing PDF/audio consumers. Daily flow opens directly on the reading; note and reflection are optional, step navigation is accessible, and the suggested action is collapsed until opened. A401 response for saved-history retrieval gives an empty history while the free reading remains visible; explicit save still requires verified authentication on the server.

Validation: deterministic chart/focus differences, exact evidence coverage, unknown-time exclusions across all four systems, inclusive contexts, Hindi copy; original chart inspector rendering and astronomical quality tests. Browser acceptance and editorial usefulness with real users remain unverified. No research-led retention or payment claims.
